Goal

Reach the glowing party door in every level. Collect vinyl for points, grab ♥ to heal, and shoot every Clanker (AI drone, bot or turret). Beat the Overfit to open the final gate. Then cut the cake in the DJ booth.

DJ Booth

Two decks mix CC0 tracks from Stopiffy, our parody streaming service: pick a track, SYNC the tempos, ride the crossfader, play with bass / treble, and loop 4 beats. Bo0m's Spotify panel embeds your own Spotify link (it can't be beat-matched; Spotify audio can't be mixed in a browser).
